Privacy

Insert Generator runs entirely in your browser. It has no accounts, no cookies and no server of its own, and counts visits only anonymously (see "Visit counts"). This page says what little is processed, and by whom.

Who is responsible

The person named in the Legal notice.

Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When your browser loads the page, GitHub receives your IP address and the usual request data (time, page, browser) and keeps them in its logs to run the service securely. The legal basis is the legitimate interest in serving the site securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See GitHub's privacy statement. Apart from the visit count below, the app itself requests nothing from any other server.

Visit counts

To learn how the app is used, it counts visits and a few actions (for example that a print file was downloaded, and in which format) with GoatCounter. It sets no cookie, and GoatCounter keeps no IP address or other personal data about you. Only a fixed name for the action, the page, the address you came from and your screen size are sent; never anything you enter, such as names or sizes, and never your inserts. Nothing is sent if your browser signals Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. The legal basis is the legitimate interest in improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Your inserts

What you enter, the box, what goes inside and the plan, is kept in this browser's storage on your device so you can come back to it. It is never sent anywhere. Files you download or open stay on your device. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes it all.

Links to other sites

Ko-fi, PayPal and GitHub links open those sites only when you click them. From then on their own privacy notices apply.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask for access to, correction or erasure of personal data about you, object to its processing, and complain to a data protection authority. This site keeps no personal data of its own, so write to the address in the Legal notice and your request will be answered or passed on to GitHub.
