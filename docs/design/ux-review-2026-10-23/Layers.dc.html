One layer control, same meaning in Top and 3D

  • The real problem: Top shows one layer, 3D shows All, and the two switches carry different labels.
  • Feedback: layer cards in component colours mix with the tray colours, and their height should not imply the layer's height.
  • Option A is the plain fix: one segment, identical labels and behaviour in both views. Option B is the strip again, now neutral and with identical card sizes; height lives in the text and a thin fill bar.
  • Either way: "Layer n" shows layers 1 to n. Top lights layer n and ghosts the ones below; 3D peels away the ones above.
