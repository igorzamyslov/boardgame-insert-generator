Board 4 · A tray is selected · chosen: docked toolbar, Top and 3D

The toolbar sits in one fixed place, whatever the view

  • A toolbar attached to the tray cannot work in 3D: the tray moves as you orbit, hides behind others, and shifts with "Spread apart". So it is docked at the bottom centre of the stage instead, in both views and on a phone.
  • Selecting a tray no longer changes the layout. Today the plan reserves a row for the bar, which makes the page jump.
  • Edit is the primary action. Pin, Rotate, Swap and Keep this size are one tap away in ⋯, each named by its effect.
  • The facts (layer, size, time) move into the dock's label, so they read as a caption, not as an action row.
