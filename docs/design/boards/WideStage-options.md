# Wide stage: three options

Board: `WideStage.dc.html` (props `option` A/B/C, `width` 2000/1440, `view` top/3d). PNGs: `png/WideStage--<option>-<width>-<view>.png`. Star Realms (101 × 195 × 37 mm), Starter deck 2 selected. Every option drops the axes button and fits the 3D model by the sphere that holds the box and every spread part, inside the frame less a 48 px side margin and a 68 px band at the top and foot, so no angle cuts it off. Distances below run from the centre of the selected tray to the centre of its actions, at 2000 × 1236. Today the actions sit about 1,000 px from the tray.

**A, the frame fills the stage.** The plan has no 900 px cap and scales to the stage's height, centred. 3D takes the whole free area under the tray bar. The bars stay full width. Travel: about 930 px in Top and 820 in 3D, so it barely helps a tall box. A wide box gains the most. Work: small (CSS for the frame, `fit.ts` margins). Phone and tablet: no change. Touches §2 Layout (`--plan-max`, "the 3D view is drawn in the same frame"), §5 The plan and The 3D view (the axes toggle, the frame's size), §2 `aria-pressed` ("Show axes"), and the §7 rows of 2026-10-19 (`--plan-max` 900) and the 3D frame rows.

**B, a working column.** The status row, tray bar and frame form one centred column, as wide as the frame and at least 900 px wide. At that width the status row wraps: the switches go under the heading, right-aligned. 3D fills the column. Travel: about 720 px in Top and 555 in 3D, because the actions are still on top and the tray can be at the box's front. Work: medium (the column, the wrapped status row, the new plan heights in `jump.mjs` and the shots). Phone: no change. Tablet: the column is the whole stage, as today. Touches §2 Layout (the stage's order and sizing, the plan's height), §5 Status heading (the segments right of the heading, slots that keep their width) and The 3D view.

**C, actions on the tray.** The bar in the slot keeps the swatch, name and line. Pin or Unpin, Turn, Edit, More and Close move into a small toolbar about 18 px beside the selected tray. In Top it sits right of the plan's frame, at the tray's height. In 3D it sits on the side where it covers no part, and it follows the tray as the model turns. The 3D view takes A's whole-stage frame. Travel: about 345 px in Top and 390 in 3D, mostly the tray's own half-width. The gap from the tray's edge is about 20 to 30 px. Work: the largest. The toolbar is anchored to a plan tile or to a projected 3D box, it must be placed to avoid collisions, it must sit after the tile in the focus order, and it must fall back to the bar where there is no room. Phone: no change (the tray is already a sheet). Tablet: needs that fallback. Touches §5 Selection and the tray bar ("Main's one wrapping row" in the slot), §2 Flow ("keep a control where it first appeared"), §5 The 3D view and the axes rules, and needs a §7 row.

**Recommendation: C.** It is the only option that puts the actions within a short reach of the tray in both views. A and B shorten the trip, but it is still more than 500 px.

## B2

Board: `WideStage2.dc.html` (props `width` 2400/1440, `mode` normal/edit, `view` top/3d). PNGs: `png/WideStage2--<width>-<normal|edit>-<top|3d>.png`. The owner's Star Realms file: Trade deck, Starter deck (3 trays in one stack, under Counter), Counter; 5 trays in 4 layers.

**Max width 1600 px.** Header content, rail and stage sit in one centred block; outside it is page colour, and the rail gets a left edge. The widest rail (480) plus B's 900 px column plus the stage's padding (72) is 1452, so at 1600 the column keeps about 75 px of air at the widest rail and 164 px each side at the default 300. Anything wider adds only empty stage and moves Download, undo and the menu away from the work. At 1440 the block fills the window.

**Status row.** Two lines in the column: the heading with Keep searching at its right, then the Layer and View switches right-aligned. Keep searching moved up from the switch line, so the switches are anchored to the column's right edge and do not move when it is absent.

**The editor's stage is the normal stage.** The sentence ("**Starter deck**, 3 trays.") takes the heading's line; the Layer and Top / 3D switches stay where they are, so the person can check an item from any layer and in 3D without leaving the editor. The item's trays are lifted and the rest dimmed in both views. There is no tray bar, but its slot stays as an empty band: the plan does not jump when an editor opens, and the band is where the problem line and the option note go. Keep searching is left out, as on the Edit board. Cost: 3D while editing would rebuild the scene on each keystroke. The mock assumes the 3D view keeps the last model and redraws it once a change settles (when the run for that change ends, as the plan does now), never per keystroke.

**DESIGN changes.** §2 Layout: a 1600 px page block (a new token, no new breakpoint), and the stage's order and sizing in a centred column. §5 Status heading: two lines, Keep searching beside the heading. §5 Editor flow: `EditorStage` keeps StageTop's switches, the View switch included, and its slot; the "status heading is not drawn while an editor is open" rule stays (the sentence is still the h1). §5 The 3D view and §2 `aria-pressed`: the axes toggle and "Show axes" go, and the fit keeps margins at any angle. §7 needs rows for the block, the editor's 3D view and the dropped axes.
