# Print referrals and tips pay the first euros

> Research report, 1 November 2026, made with web research for the question "how can this app earn a small passive income". Read with [DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md) in mind: the report's Treatstock _pre-load_ (uploading the 3MF from the browser) is a runtime request to another host, which the "Hosting and network" rule forbids today and which sends the person's file to a third party. A plain link, like the Ko-fi one, stays within the rule; the pre-load needs a deliberate decision in the decision log first. The Impressum and privacy page would go into the project menu, next to "Licences", since the app has no footer.

Research state: 1 November 2026. "Confirmed" means read on the programme's or authority's own page; "inference" and "estimate" are marked as such throughout.

For a free, static, client-only insert generator the money is in four places, in this order of effort-to-return: a "No printer? Have it printed" link to **Treatstock (up to 5 % of the order, and the only service whose API can pre-load the generated file)** and **JLC3DP ($5 cash per converted customer per month)**; a labelled filament/printer affiliate link (**Amazon pays 3 % on filament and printers in DE/UK and 2.5–4 % in the US; Prusa 3 %, Creality 3–8 %, Elegoo/eSUN from 5 %**, i.e. €0.30–1 per spool and €9–48 per printer); the Ko-fi/PayPal tip that is already in the download dialog (**Ko-fi charges 0 % on one-off tips; the best-documented average gift for donation-funded free software is $16.90 with a median of $11.12**); and, last, publishing printed-and-photographed insert packs on MakerWorld, Printables or Cults3D, which is real but not passive. **Craftcloud, the obvious "have it printed" candidate, has no affiliate programme at all** and only hands out 10 % discount codes. Summed across all channels, a rough planning figure is **€40–150 per 1,000 downloads**, an estimate built from affiliate rates, order-size anchors and nonprofit conversion benchmarks, not from any measured insert-tool data. Before the first euro arrives the owner must do more paperwork than code: a Gewerbeanmeldung (€15–60) and the ELSTER tax questionnaire within a month, an Impressum with a street address, a Datenschutzerklärung that names GitHub's IP logging, an "Anzeige"/"Werbung" label on every affiliate link plus Amazon's verbatim sentence "Als Amazon-Partner verdiene ich an qualifizierten Verkäufen", a written note to the employer, and the programme sign-ups themselves (Treatstock contact form, JLC3DP application and contract, Amazon PartnerNet with its three-sales-in-180-days gate, Awin with a €1 verification fee). Analytics can be added without a consent banner using Cloudflare's script-free edge counts or a GoatCounter image pixel, but no German authority has blessed any specific tool, so this is defensible rather than confirmed.

## Treatstock and JLC3DP pay for "have it printed"; Craftcloud pays nothing

The "No printer?" link is the channel with no competitor precedent and the only one that fits a tool whose users leave with a file in hand. Of the services examined, **Treatstock** is the only one that combines a cash payout with a deep-linkable upload. Its marketing page says "50% of our earning is yours" and that withdrawal is via PayPal, while its help centre states affiliates receive "up to 5% for orders placed by referrals", provided the order is not cancelled or refunded, with referred accounts tracked for up to six months after sign-up ([Treatstock Affiliate Reward Program](https://www.treatstock.com/l/affiliates); [Treatstock help 114](https://www.treatstock.com/help/article/114-become-an-affiliate); [Treatstock help 85](https://www.treatstock.co.uk/help/article/85-referral-tracker)). Joining is a contact form ("Join Affiliate Program"); the help centre lists exactly three affiliate articles and refers payout questions to support@treatstock.com ([Treatstock affiliate help category](https://www.treatstock.com/help/category/14-treatstock-affiliate-program)). The integration matters more than the rate: the v2 API accepts `POST /api/v2/printable-packs` with `files[]` or `files-urls[]` (STL, PLY, 3MF) and an optional `location[country]`, and returns a `redir` link to a pre-loaded model page valid 24 hours, plus `widgetUrl`/`widgetHtml` for an iframe; a restricted "public-upload-key" exists for client-side uploads, and the pack endpoints expose an `affiliate_price` field ([Treatstock API docs](https://www.treatstock.co.uk/help/article/83-api-documentation)). The API partner page promises that "every successful order through the API will generate an additional source of income for your website" without stating a percentage ([Treatstock help 113](https://www.treatstock.com/help/article/113-become-an-api-partner)). Inference: the public-upload-key lets the browser app upload the generated 3MF itself and open the returned `redir` page, so the user never re-uploads and attribution survives; the 24-hour validity means the link must be generated at click time, not stored in a project file. Two caveats are confirmed gaps: Treatstock's own service-fee percentage is unpublished (the "50 % of our earning" and "up to 5 %" statements are consistent only if the fee is about 10 %), and the API examples carry a 2019 timestamp, so endpoint behaviour in 2026 was not verifiable without a key.

**JLC3DP** is the only large service with published cash terms. Its affiliate article (last updated 28 Oct 2025, read from the zh-HK mirror because the English page rendered empty) requires an existing account, an online application, e-mail approval and a signed affiliate contract, and then pays, for "each customer who completes an order through your tracking link each month", **an $8 coupon or a $5 cash reward**, issued monthly; links use a `?from=` parameter ([JLC3DP affiliate program (zh-HK)](https://jlc3dp.com/hk/help/article/affiliate-program)). This is a flat bounty, so order size is irrelevant, and JLC3DP's FDM envelope (PLA-P max 250 × 250 × 300 mm) fits 200 mm trays ([JLC3DP FDM page](https://jlc3dp.com/3d-printing/fused-deposition-modeling)); JLC3DP cut PLA-P prices 18 % on 24 Jul 2026 ([JLC3DP pricing update July 2026](https://jlc3dp.com/news/materials-finishing-pricing-update-july2026)). Its separate referral scheme pays coupons only, which "cannot be redeemed for cash" ([JLC referral program terms](https://jlc3dp.com/help/article/referral-program-terms)). Payout method, minimum and attribution window are not published.

**Craftcloud** (All3DP GmbH) should be understood as a free convenience, not a channel: its site has no affiliate, API or developer link, `/affiliate-program` returns HTTP 404, and the only referral mechanism is a "Refer a Friend" scheme whose reward is a 10 % discount code on both sides ([Craftcloud home](https://craftcloud3d.com/en); [Craftcloud KB art. 212](https://support.craftcloud3d.com/en/articles/212-how-can-i-find-special-offers-or-discounts-for-my-craftcloud-order)). Searches of Awin, Impact, ShareASale, Tradedoubler and Belboon found no Craftcloud listing, and no URL parameter pre-loads a file into `/en/upload`. **PCBWay**'s "Portals" affiliate page (last updated 2018) promises "a commission" with no rate, cookie or payout stated, and its referral scheme pays a $10 coupon per converted friend ([PCBWay affiliate help](https://www.pcbway.com/helpcenter/promotions/Do_you_have_an_Affiliate_Program_.html); [PCBWay referral](https://www.pcbway.com/helpcenter/promotions/How_does_the_referral_program_work_.html)); the "5 % commission" figure circulating on Hackster and Hackaday is third-party and unverified. **Sculpteo**'s 2016 programme (7 % or 5 %, 25-day cookie) is gone: the affiliate page returns "Page not Found" and the marketplace closed on 4 Nov 2023 ([Sculpteo affiliate page](https://www.sculpteo.com/en/affiliate-program/); [Digital Engineering](https://www.digitalengineering247.com/article/sculpteo-shutters-3d-printing-marketplace-after-10-plus-years)). **i.materialise** stopped taking orders on 31 Oct 2025 and its referral-API pages now redirect or 404 ([Fabbaloo](https://www.fabbaloo.com/news/i-materialise-to-cease-orders-by-october-31-as-materialise-consolidates-3d-print-services); [i.materialise API business models](https://i.materialise.com/api/business-models)). Xometry, Protolabs Network (ex-Hubs) and the relaunched Shapeways have no findable referral programme, and the only German print-service programme found, Protiq's €100-per-new-customer scheme on Webgains, is listed as closed ([affi.io: Protiq DE](https://affi.io/m/protiq-de)).

How much is a commission worth per order? No service publishes a per-gram PLA price and nobody has quoted parts of this size, so the anchors are indirect: All3DP's 2026 test priced four small PLA brackets at Craftcloud at €20.95 plus €10.98 shipping ([All3DP FR, updated 9 Feb 2026](https://all3dp.com/fr/1/5-technologies-dimpression-3d-comparees-laquelle-offre-le-meilleur-resultat-sur-les-services-en-ligne/)); Treatstock's API example shows $5.74 minimum for a 27.5 g PLA part (2019 example) ([Treatstock API docs](https://www.treatstock.co.uk/help/article/83-api-documentation)); PCBWay's FAQ lists a $25 minimum order and $5–20 material start-up cost ([PCBWay CNC FAQ](https://www.pcbway.com/rapid-prototyping/cnc-faq.html)). Estimate: scaling these to 500–900 g of trays gives a very rough **€60–250 per order before shipping**, so Treatstock's "up to 5 %" is **€3–12 per order**, JLC3DP a flat **$5**, Craftcloud **€0**. Shipping (about half the bill on a small order in All3DP's test) is unlikely to be commissionable. The only way to get a real number is to upload one generated 3MF to each service, which this research did not do.

## Filament links earn cents, printer links earn euros, and Amazon gates you on three sales

A "Get filament" link beside the estimated grams is cheap to add and worth little per click. **Amazon.de** pays **3.0 % under "Alle anderen Kategorien"** (filament, printers, Computer and Elektronik are not listed separately; page fetched 1 Nov 2026, no effective date shown), and amazon.co.uk mirrors this at 3.0 % ([partnernet.amazon.de compensation](https://partnernet.amazon.de/welcome/compensation); [affiliate-program.amazon.co.uk compensation](https://affiliate-program.amazon.co.uk/welcome/compensation)). Amazon.com pays 3 % on Business & Industrial Supplies and Tools, 2.5 % on PC components and 4 % on "All Other"; Lasso re-checked the identical table on 14 Oct 2026 ([affiliate-program.amazon.com compensation](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/welcome/compensation); [Lasso, 2026-10-14](https://getlasso.co/amazon-affiliate-commission-rate/)). Inference: filament and printers land at 3 % in DE/UK and at 2.5–4 % in the US, because Amazon does not publish the browse-node-to-category mapping. Three further Amazon rules shape the implementation. Each marketplace is a separate account, and each is approved only after "at least three [qualified sales] within the first 180 days", with personal orders excluded and no reinstatement after rejection ([Amazon Application Review Process](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/G8TW5AE9XL2VX9VM)); OneLink lets a US-primary account add DE, UK, FR, IT, ES and CA, but the help page does not document a Germany-primary account adding the US ([Amazon OneLink help](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/G2L3ZBRGXTS7EMEY)). The session cookie is 24 hours, with cart items still qualifying if ordered within 89 days ([Amazon Associates linking policy](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/linking)). And on the EU5 programmes a link that lands on a search or category page rather than a product detail page earns only the lower "indirect" rate, whose percentage is not visible on the public pages ([Geniuslink, 2026-05-18](https://geniuslink.com/blog/direct-qualifying-vs-indirect-qualifying-purchases/)); the quantity the app computes ("about 800 g") can only live in the link text, since no programme accepts a quantity parameter. Amazon's German Programmrichtlinien permit links in "Online-Softwareanwendungen" but forbid them in client-side software, framed Amazon pages, and anything that hides the referring URL ([PartnerNet Teilnahmebedingungen](https://partnernet.amazon.de/help/operating/agreement); [PartnerNet Programmrichtlinien](https://partnernet.amazon.de/help/operating/participation)); inference: links belong in the running web app, never inside exported 3MF/PDF/plan files, and never behind a shortener. Non-US associates must complete the tax interview that produces a W-8BEN with a treaty claim or face up to 30 % US withholding ([Amazon: non-US person](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GPFZ6W6CF4E5BD9V)). Payout minimums for DE (€25 direct deposit per Geniuslink, Feb 2024) were not confirmed on an Amazon page ([Geniuslink Germany guide](https://geniuslink.com/blog/amazon-associates-germany/)). Shopifreaks reported in May 2026 that Amazon privately cut some publishers' rates by up to 50 %, but the public standard table is unchanged ([Shopifreaks, 2026-05-21](https://www.shopifreaks.com/amazon-quietly-slashed-associates-affiliate-commission-rates-by-up-to-50-across-publisher-ecosystem-and-gutted-reporting-tools/)).

Manufacturer and retailer programmes pay more per sale and have friendlier cookies, and none excludes German publishers. **Prusa** runs its own portal at **3 % with 30-day attribution**, monthly payouts, "worldwide" ([partner.prusa3d.com](https://partner.prusa3d.com/)). **Creality** is on Impact at **3–8 %, 30-day cookie**, and on Awin at 1–8 % with DE/EU/UK storefronts listed ([Impact: Creality](https://app.impact.com/advertiser-advertiser-info/CrealityOfficialStore.brand); [Awin: Creality](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile/97013)). **Elegoo** moved to Impact in 2025 and sits on Awin at **from 5 %, 30-day cookie**, with a Germany storefront ([Awin: Elegoo](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile/61127)). **eSUN** is on Awin at **from 5 %, 30-day cookie** ([Awin: eSUN](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile/99267)). **Bambu Lab** is on Awin (merchant 46345) with a **15-day** attribution window and rates visible only after approval ([Awin: Bambu Lab](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile/46345)). Anycubic advertises "from 5 %" with a 60-day cookie on its Australian page, snippet-level only. **Polymaker** advertises the highest filament rates, "up to 20 %" (US page) or 25 % (Canadian page), but every Polymaker page returned HTTP 429, so this is unverified ([shop.polymaker.com affiliates](https://shop.polymaker.com/pages/affiliates-program)). Two European filament sellers publish rates on Awin: Redline Filament DE at 5 % and AzureFilm at 10 %, both with 30-day cookies ([Awin: Redline Filament DE](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile/22266); [Awin: AzureFilm](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile/106471)). Among German retailers, **3DJake** runs separate DE and FR programmes on Awin and requires an own website "in the 3D-printing field", with the rate shown only after approval ([3djake.de Affiliate-Programm](https://www.3djake.de/info/affiliate-programm)); Conrad is on Awin with a 30-day cookie, rate unpublished ([Awin: Conrad DE](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile/11467)). No programme was found for Sunlu, Hatchbox, Overture, Filamentworld, Das Filament or Printed Solid. The networks themselves are German-friendly: Awin AG is a German company, charges a refundable €1 verification fee at sign-up and pays out from a €20 EUR threshold twice monthly ([Awin: payment thresholds](https://success.awin.com/articles/en_US/Knowledge/What-are-the-payment-thresholds); [Awin: sign-up fee](https://success.awin.com/articles/en_US/Knowledge/Why-do-I-have-to-pay-the-sign-up-fee)); Impact pays from $10 by bank or PayPal but blocks payouts until a tax form is approved ([Impact: how partners get paid](https://help.impact.com/partner/what-would-you-like-to-learn-about/platform-features/finance/payments-withdrawals-and-balance/how-do-partners-get-paid)).

The arithmetic is unforgiving for filament and kind to printers. A 1 kg PLA spool sells for $18–24 in the US (Snapmaker, 26 May 2026) and budget brands in Germany run €7–13 per kg ([Snapmaker, 2026-05-26](https://www.snapmaker.com/blog/how-much-is-filament-for-a-3d-printer/); [china-gadgets.de](https://www.china-gadgets.de/?p=236167)). Inference: Amazon's 3 % on a €20 spool is about **€0.60**, a manufacturer's 5 % about **€1**, AzureFilm's 10 % about **€2**; Prusa's 3 % on a ~€1,000 printer is about **€30**, Creality's 3–8 % on €300–600 is **€9–48**, Amazon's 2.5–4 % on a €400 printer **€10–16**. One printer sale equals fifteen to fifty spool sales, so a quiet "Don't have a printer?" link aimed at the user who is about to use a print service anyway is the better affiliate bet. There is no published conversion benchmark for utility sites; Lasso puts Amazon affiliate conversion at a broad 1–10 % by niche ([Lasso](https://getlasso.co/amazon-affiliate-conversion-rate-3/)), and a casual-browsing figure of 3–5 % comes from a marketing calculator, not measurement ([affiliatexblocks](https://affiliatexblocks.com/?p=27609)). Inference: reaching Amazon's three-sales gate in 180 days needs on the order of 100+ clicks per marketplace in that window, which a low-traffic tool may miss in one or more marketplaces; Awin/Impact programmes have no such gate.

German disclosure wording is settled and strict. Affiliate links are "kommerzielle Kommunikation" under § 6 Abs. 1 Nr. 1 DDG and advertising under § 5a Abs. 4 UWG; the IT-Recht Kanzlei (updated 13 Nov 2025) states that **"Anzeige" or "Werbung" directly at the link** suffices, an asterisk with a same-page explanation is acceptable where space is short, and "Affiliate-Link", "sponsored by", "unterstützt von" and "gesponsert" are insufficient per BGH I ZR 2/11 ([IT-Recht Kanzlei](https://www.it-recht-kanzlei.de/affiliate-links-korrekte-kennzeichnung.html); [gesetze-im-internet.de § 6 DDG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ddg/__6.html)). LG Berlin held a shopping-cart icon alone insufficient, and OLG München (30 Oct 2025, 29 U 2633/24 e) extended the duty to teasers that lead to affiliate content ([IT-Recht Kanzlei: LG Berlin](https://www.it-recht-kanzlei.de/lgberlin-affiliate-einkaufswagen.html); [OLG München 30.10.2025](https://www.gesetze-bayern.de/Content/Document/Y-300-Z-GRURRS-B-2025-N-38058?hl=true)). Amazon's German agreement (15 Oct 2025) additionally requires the verbatim sentence **"Als Amazon-Partner verdiene ich an qualifizierten Verkäufen"** to appear "klar und deutlich" on the site ([PartnerNet Teilnahmevereinbarung](https://partnernet.amazon.de/help/operating/agreement)). Inference: for a single link inside the download dialog the safe pattern is the link text followed by "Anzeige", with Amazon's sentence as the footnote under it and repeated in the Impressum; no case law exists on labelling inside a web-app dialog, and the "obviously commercial" exception of § 5a Abs. 4 UWG is untested there, so label anyway.

## Marketplaces reward printed photos, not generator output

Publishing generated insert packs is the channel with the most moving parts and the least passivity. On the paid side, **Cults3D** pays creators 80 % of the net price excluding VAT and applies VAT only to its own commission because it acts as a transparent intermediary ([Cults3D homepage](https://cults3d.com/en); [Cults3D terms](https://cults3d.com/it/legale)). **Printables Store** takes 20 % plus Stripe fees, pays the creator directly through Stripe, sets a $5 minimum price and starts with one paid slot ([Prusa blog: Printables Store](https://blog.prusa3d.com/launching-printables-store_87810/)). **MyMiniFactory** charges 15 % at the entry tier plus 3.49 % + $0.49 processing and currently 0 % payout fee ([MMF Store Manager fees](https://creator.myminifactory.com/store-manager-fees)). **CGTrader** cut new sellers to 55 % on 15 Sept 2026 ([CGTrader forum announcement](https://www.cgtrader.com/forum/general-discussions/payout-rate-update-effective-september-15-2026)). **Etsy** stacks a $0.20 listing fee per item per four months, 6.5 % transaction fee, 4 % + €0.30 processing for a German bank account, a regulatory operating fee since 22 Jun 2026 and 12–15 % Offsite Ads on attributed orders ([Etsy processing fees by country](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015628847); [beancount.io, Aug 2026](https://beancount.io/de/blog/2026/08/22/etsy-regulatory-operating-fee-increase-june-2026-seller-payout-bookkeeping-guide)). Price anchors seen in Nov 2026: Etsy STL insert packs at $3.87–12.55, printed inserts at $49.99–96.26, the Etsy shop PrintMyInsert with 454 lifetime sales at a $37.99 median, and a Shopify "Dragon Eclipse" STL pack at $48 flagged "sold 100+ in last 30 days" (snippet only, unverified) ([Etsy market: boardgame insert stl](https://www.etsy.com/market/boardgame_insert_stl); [makerwords: PrintMyInsert](https://www.makerwords.com/shop/printmyinsert); [shop.app listing](https://shop.app/products/10910622843147/dragon-eclipse-board-game-insert-stl-files-only)). Inference: for a Kleinunternehmer, Cults3D and Printables Store are administratively simplest; Etsy's fixed fees make sub-€5 files marginal; who is the seller of record for VAT on Cults/MMF (deemed-supplier rules) was not found and belongs on a Steuerberater's desk if sales start.

On the free side, only **MakerWorld** converts to cash, and only through Exclusive Points at **$0.066 per point with a $100 minimum** (about 1,516 points), paid via Airwallex after manual review; joining needs an account with 100 total prints ([Bambu Lab blog: Exclusive Model Program](https://blog.bambulab.com/exclusive-model-program-cash-rewards-and-copyright-support); [BambuHub, updated 7 Oct 2026](https://bambuhub.net/guides/makerworld-points-rewards)). Regular points convert to Bambu gift cards at roughly $0.08 per point (community-reported). Since 16 Jun 2025 the points formula is undisclosed and weights originality, complexity and presentation ("photos, verified print profiles, instructions"), with the explicit warning that "endless low-effort duplication is not rewarded" ([Bambu forum: points system upgrade](https://forum.bambulab.com/t/why-we-re-upgrading-our-points-system/172869)); creators report that parametric models "earn poorly" and that Customize-button downloads are not counted ([Bambu forum: Customize downloads](https://forum.bambulab.com/t/downloads-from-parametric-models-customize-button-do-not-appear-to-be-counted/256884)). A German Kleinunternehmer has cashed out $1,000; business accounts need an invoice and VAT-ID, and a user without VAT-ID was told to withdraw as a personal account ([Bambu forum: Redeem points for cash](https://forum.bambulab.com/t/redeem-points-for-cash-process/133394)). **Printables** pays Prusameters only: 200 per month for a model reaching 400 downloads and 20 likes in that month, with **350 Prusameters = one Prusament spool voucher**, no cash, and upload rules that demand your own design and photos of your own print ([Prusa help: Prusameters](https://help.prusa3d.com/article/how-do-prusameters-on-printables-work_365065)). Thangs has no points programme and charges 14 % plus 4 % tax processing on memberships ([Thangs: charges](https://thangs.com/resources/help-center-articles/what-does-thangs-charge-designers)). Demand exists but is thin per game: the biggest free Wingspan inserts show low-thousands counts (2,291 on Printables, "1.1k" on MakerWorld, labels unclear in snippets) ([Printables model 151861](https://www.printables.com/model/151861-wingspan-box-insert-for-core-european-oceania-and-); [MakerWorld model 841751](https://makerworld.com/models/841751)). Inference: a model at the top Printables tier earns about 0.57 spools a month (roughly €15–20 in vouchers); on MakerWorld a boost is 12–15 points, about $1, so income scales with boosts from a small audience. Both platforms require a real print photo per listing, which means one physical print per game (community inserts weigh 300–1,250 g), so the "passive" part only begins after that outlay.

Naming the game is legally defensible if done plainly. In the EU, Art. 14(1)(c) EUTMR permits use of a mark to indicate intended purpose "in particular as accessories or spare parts", subject to honest practices, with Gillette (2005) as the compatibility precedent and Audi v GQ (C-334/22, 2024) marking the limit at reproducing the mark's own shape ([Fieldfisher on Audi v GQ](https://www.fieldfisher.com/en/services/intellectual-property/intellectual-property-blog/running-rings-around-referential-use-of-trade-marks-the-cjeus-decision-in-audi-v-gq)). In the US, nominative fair use covers the same ground, with a game-law firm warning against making the game name larger than your own brand, using its logo, or words like "official" ([Legal Moves, 27 Dec 2024](https://legalmoveslawfirm.com/game-trays-accessories-other-games/)). Stonemaier states that third-party accessory makers "follow these guidelines and don't pay any royalties to us" ([Stonemaier Games](https://stonemaiergames.com/?p=12278)). Sellers in the wild write "in no way affiliated or licensed by Stonemaier Games" and "the 'Wingspan' trademark is owned by Stonemaier LLC and the term is used only to describe compatibility" ([Etsy listing 1012755829](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1012755829)); German sellers add "(inoffizielles Produkt)". Inference: title listings "Insert compatible with <Game>", plain text, no box art, logo or font, your own brand first, and a one-line non-affiliation disclaimer naming the mark owner. The practical risk is platform takedowns rather than court: MakerWorld removed a UNO box design at Mattel's request on 19 Mar 2026 and deducts points after removal ([Bambu forum: IP line](https://forum.bambulab.com/t/where-exactly-is-the-ip-line-for-designers-here/259637)), though no takedown of a board-game insert by a board-game publisher was found anywhere.

## Tips already in the dialog: zero-fee on Ko-fi, ~€10 average, ask once

No free web tool publishes a visitor-to-donor rate, so the donation channel must be planned from proxies. The cleanest data is Thunderbird's: in 2023 it collected $8.6 M from over 300,000 contributors, **average $16.90, median $11.12**, 61 % of gifts $20 or less, and Germany its top giving country ([Thunderbird annual report 2023-2024, 2 Oct 2024](https://blog.thunderbird.net/2024/10/thunderbird-annual-report-2023-2024/)). Wikimedia's average across all sources was **$10.15 in FY2024-25** ([Wikimedia Fundraising 2024-25 Report](https://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Fundraising/2024-25_Report)). Nonprofit websites convert roughly 1.2–1.6 % of all visitors into donors, 11 % desktop / 8 % mobile on the donation form itself ([Rafirit citing Neon One](https://rafirit.com/blog-resources/how-to-improve-nonprofit-website-donation-conversion-rate/); [Blackbaud](https://blog.blackbaud.com/two-must-know-metrics-to-supercharge-your-donation-forms/)). Excalidraw, a top-tier free browser tool, has raised $38,539.70 on Open Collective since 2020 with an estimated annual budget of $2,191 ([Open Collective: Excalidraw](https://opencollective.com/excalidraw)). Inference: a hobby tool that asks once at the download moment should plan on **0.1–0.5 % of downloaders tipping, at €10–17 each**; this is a planning assumption, not a measured figure.

Fees favour the existing Ko-fi/PayPal set-up. Ko-fi's free plan charges **0 % on one-off tips** and 5 % on shop, commission and membership sales, with Ko-fi Gold at $12/month for new creators; these figures come from a 2026 third-party review because ko-fi.com itself blocked fetching (HTTP 403), and older sources quote $6–8 for Gold ([Built by Foundry, Ko-fi review, updated 25 Jul 2026](https://www.builtbyfoundry.io/blog/ko-fi-review-2026-is-it-worth-it)). Buy Me a Coffee charges 5 % with no monthly fee ([buymeacoffee.com/faq](https://www.buymeacoffee.com/faq)). GitHub Sponsors charges 0 % on sponsorships from personal accounts and supports Germany as a payout region ([GitHub Docs: Sponsors billing](https://docs.github.com/en/billing/concepts/third-party-payments/github-sponsors)). PayPal Germany's merchant fee list of 15 Jul 2026 sets goods-and-services receipts at **2.49 % + €0.35**, with international surcharges of 0 % EEA, 1.29 % UK, 1.99 % US/Canada and 2.99 % elsewhere; the "Spende" rate of 2.49 % applies only under PayPal's fundraiser terms ([PayPal DE merchant fees PDF, 15 Jul 2026](https://www.paypalobjects.com/marketing/ua/pdf/DE/de/de-de-merchant-fees-15-jul-2026.pdf)). Inference: a €10 tip nets about €9.40 through Ko-fi-via-PayPal or direct PayPal and about €9.15 through Buy Me a Coffee; Ko-fi Gold only pays off above roughly $240/month of shop revenue, which is irrelevant here.

The behavioural evidence on wording and amounts is consistent. Goswami and Urminsky (JMR 2016, 11,508 participants) found that low defaults raise participation but shrink gifts, that defaults are "self-canceling" with no net revenue effect on average, and that 76 % of the fifty largest US charities used no default ([Chicago Booth summary](https://www.chicagobooth.edu/research/rustandy/social-impact-research/research-papers/2016/when-should-the-ask-be-a-nudge-the-effect-of-default-amounts-on-charitable-donations)). Duke's Center for Advanced Hindsight tested $5/$30/$55 defaults: $30 "led more people to give compared to both the $5 and $55 defaults" while $55 pushed many to give nothing ([Advanced Hindsight](https://advanced-hindsight.com/blog/behavioral-scientists-guide-increasing-charitable-donations/)). Wikimedia found only 3–5 % of donations arrive after a device's tenth banner impression ([Wikimedia Fundraising 2016-17 Report](https://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Fundraising/2016-17_Report)). Inference: offer two or three presets with a mid-level default (€5/€10/€20 with €10 preselected) plus a free field, ask once at the completion moment the dialog already occupies, and never nag. German tax law adds a wording constraint: FG Berlin-Brandenburg (12 Jun 2025, 14 K 14067/24) held that reader payments solicited by a free blog are taxable Betriebseinnahmen regardless of being called donations, with BFH appeals VIII R 18/25 and V R 10/25 pending ([beck-aktuell, 3 Dec 2025](https://www.beck-aktuell.de/heute-im-recht/rechtsprechung/fg-berlin-brandenburg-14-k-14067-24-blogger-spenden-einnahmen-einkommensteuer-2025-12-03); [ETL, 24 Mar 2026](https://www.etl.de/aktuelles/donations-an-influencer-2026-steuerpflichtig/)). Inference: avoid "Spende"/"spenden" and anything implying a Spendenquittung; "Unterstützen", "Projekt unterstützen" or Ko-fi's own "Buy me a coffee" is accurate, and every receipt goes into the EÜR gross with fees as expenses.

## Cloudflare edge counts and a GoatCounter pixel need no banner, but nobody has said so officially

The analytics question in Germany turns on § 25 TDDDG, which requires consent for storing information on, or accessing information already in, the user's device unless strictly necessary ([gesetze-im-internet.de § 25 TDDDG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ttdsg/__25.html)). The authorities' own guidance, DSK OH Digitale Dienste v1.2 (Nov 2024), refuses a blanket verdict: Rn. 87 says no statement on whether reach measurement is consent-free "finden sich in dieser Orientierungshilfe"; Rn. 88 describes the consent-free model as a pure page counter, log-file counting without personal data, or "ein einfaches Zählpixel (des direkt aufgerufenen digitalen Dienstes)" that captures no further user data, and contrasts it with services that record "Downloads und Eingaben"; Rn. 24 treats active JavaScript read-out of device properties for fingerprinting as "access" ([DSK OH Digitale Dienste PDF](https://datenschutzkonferenz-online.de/media/oh/OH_Digitale_Dienste.pdf)). activeMind.legal (Dec 2024) concludes that IP-based reach measurement "bedarf regelmäßig keiner Einwilligung" as long as device properties are not actively transmitted ([activeMind.legal](https://www.activemind.legal/de/guides/edsa-reichweitenmessung/)), while Matomo itself says only server-side methods are "clearly recognised as outside the scope" and that cookie-less JavaScript should be treated as consent-requiring "unless and until a supervisory authority provides clarity" ([Matomo FAQ](https://matomo.org/faq/how-do-i-use-matomo-analytics-without-consent-or-cookie-banner/)). The EU Digital Omnibus would add an explicit "first-party aggregated audience measurement" exemption; the Council agreed its mandate on 21 Oct 2026 and the file is in trilogue, not law ([Heuking, 23 Oct 2026](https://www.heuking.de/en/news-events/newsletter-articles/detail/cookie-consent-under-the-digital-omnibus-planned-changes-and-technical-implementation.html)).

Ranked by exposure, lowest first (inference from the DSK text), the options are these. **Cloudflare zone analytics** runs nothing on the device: proxying a custom domain through Cloudflare's Free plan yields Requests, Bandwidth and Unique Visitors for the last 30 days, no script, no code, but no custom events ([Cloudflare zone analytics](https://developers.cloudflare.com/analytics/account-and-zone-analytics/zone-analytics/)). **GoatCounter** is free for reasonable public use, never stores IP or User-Agent (sessions are an in-memory hash mapped to a UUID for 8 hours), self-describes as "probably" not needing a GDPR notice, and offers a no-JavaScript image pixel that can also record an event via `?p=download-3mf&e=true` ([goatcounter.com](https://www.goatcounter.com/); [GoatCounter pixel](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/pixel); [GoatCounter sessions](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/sessions)). Counting a download click is exactly the kind of extra evaluation the DSK lists beyond pure counting, so this is one notch riskier than a bare page pixel. **Umami Cloud** is free up to 100k events a month with custom events, MIT-licensed for self-hosting ([umami.is/pricing](https://umami.is/pricing)); **Plausible** is $9/month at 10k pageviews, EU-only processing, AGPL self-host free ([plausible.io pricing](https://plausible.io/#pricing); [Plausible data policy, Oct 2026](https://plausible.io/data-policy)); **Fathom** $15/month with a card required and **Simple Analytics** free with a badge or €20/month ([usefathom.com/pricing](https://usefathom.com/pricing); [simpleanalytics.com/pricing](https://www.simpleanalytics.com/pricing)); Cloudflare Web Analytics' beacon is free but documents no event API ([Cloudflare Web Analytics FAQ](https://developers.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/faq/)). All script-based tools actively read screen size, referrer and language. Inference: the zero-cost, lowest-risk configuration is Cloudflare edge counts for traffic plus a GoatCounter pixel or one-line script for the download event, described in the Datenschutzerklärung under Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. f DSGVO with an AV-Vertrag and an objection route; no banner is needed because nothing is stored on the device, but this is "defensible", not "authority-confirmed", and no German authority has named any of these tools either way. Whether a proxied GitHub Pages custom domain behaves as a full Cloudflare zone for edge counts was inferred from Cloudflare's generic docs, not from a GitHub-Pages-specific source.

## Research gaps that still need the owner's own tests

The largest gap is the price of printing a real insert: no service publishes per-gram PLA pricing and none was tested with 100 × 200 × 40 mm trays, so the €60–250 order anchor, and therefore the Treatstock commission, rests on scaling small-part tests; one upload per service would settle it. Treatstock's service-fee percentage, payout minimum, schedule, tax forms and German eligibility are unpublished, as are JLC3DP's payout method, attribution window and whether the $5 bounty recurs monthly per customer or is paid once; both must be asked by e-mail. Craftcloud's willingness to strike a bespoke tracked-link deal is unknown, and PCBWay's Portals rate is unpublished. On affiliates, Amazon's logged-in commission statements and the DE/UK "indirect" rate could not be fetched, the DE payout minimums rest on a 2024 Geniuslink article, Bambu Lab's and 3DJake's rates are visible only after approval, and Polymaker's 20–25 % is a blocked-page snippet. On marketplaces, Cults3D's withdrawal minimum and method, MyMiniFactory's payout details, MakerWorld's official gift-card table and exclusivity definition, Thangs' country support, and the VAT seller-of-record status on Cults and MMF for a German Kleinunternehmer were not found; no first-hand earnings report from an insert designer was retrievable because Reddit and BGG were inaccessible. Ko-fi's official fee and Gold pricing pages blocked fetching, so the 0 %-on-tips and $12/month figures are from 2026 third-party reviews that conflict with older $6–8 figures. No German supervisory authority has named GoatCounter, Plausible, Umami, Fathom, Simple Analytics or Cloudflare Web Analytics as consent-free or consent-requiring, GitHub's current Privacy Statement on Pages log retention was not fetched, and the Cloudflare-proxied-GitHub-Pages edge-count claim is inferred from generic zone docs. On the legal side, Berufsgenossenschaft duties for a one-person Nebengewerbe, how a Kleinunternehmer reports Amazon commissions in the annual VAT return, § 19 Abs. 2 UStG's definition of Gesamtumsatz for foreign-place-of-supply services, and whether a non-charity may use the word "Spende" under the UWG were not resolved; the BFH's pending VIII R 18/25 and V R 10/25 may change the donation picture in 2026–27. Finally, no conversion benchmark exists for utility sites, so every per-1,000-downloads figure is an assumption to be replaced by the owner's own counts.

## Conclusion

The research reframes the question from "which channel pays best" to "which channel nobody else in this niche has tried". Every competing generator is free and unmonetised, the marketplaces reward physical prints and photos rather than generated files, and brand money flows to video creators and platform-hosted models, so the two levers that are specific to a browser tool whose user leaves with a file are a tip asked once at the download moment and a print-service hand-off that pre-loads that file. Treatstock is the only service that does both the pre-load and the payout, which makes the owner's existing architecture (client-only, no backend) an advantage rather than a limitation: the public-upload-key lets the browser do the upload, and the attribution lives in the returned link, not in a server.

The second reframing is that the first euro is gated by paperwork, not code, and that the paperwork is cheap but not optional: a Gewerbeanmeldung for a site that may earn €30 a month looks disproportionate, yet German law has no floor, the Impressum duty starts with the first affiliate link, and the 2025 Berlin-Brandenburg ruling makes even "donations" business income. Doing the registrations once, in week one, converts every later channel into a bookkeeping line rather than a legal question. From there the owner's own analytics, not any benchmark in this report, will decide whether the €40–150 per 1,000 downloads planning figure was pessimistic or generous, and the only way to shrink the widest uncertainty, the price of a printed insert, is to upload one generated 3MF to Treatstock and JLC3DP and read the quote.
